# Poker Vision — Google Play / IARC Content Rating Preflight

Poker Vision teaches Texas Hold'em hand rankings and calculates poker probabilities. It does **not** support real-money wagering, deposits, withdrawals, prizes, gambling operators, betting links, or gambling advertising.

## IARC / Play Console questionnaire
When the questionnaire asks about casinos, gambling halls, racetracks, simulated gambling, or games commonly played for money, answer according to the actual wording shown in Play Console. Google Play's content-rating guidance explicitly says this category includes games that teach users how to play card games that are usually played for money or how to play odds.

Therefore do **not** answer "No" merely because Poker Vision has no real-money wagering. Disclose the poker/card-game educational content accurately and let IARC determine the regional age rating.

## Real-money gambling policy
Poker Vision is positioned as an educational/offline analysis tool and must remain outside real-money gambling functionality:
- no wager or stake entry
- no deposits or withdrawals
- no cash or real-world prizes
- no links/calls to action to betting operators
- no integrated gambling ads
- no player funds or payout management

## Target audience
Recommended Play target audience: adults / 18+ only. Do not enroll the app in Designed for Families.

## Store listing consistency
Keep these statements visible and consistent:
- Educational/offline poker analysis tool
- No real-money betting, deposits, withdrawals, or cash prizes
- Do not use electronic assistance where venue, tournament, casino, or online-service rules prohibit it
