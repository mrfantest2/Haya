## English

### Short description
Scan cards, learn poker hands, and estimate offline Texas Hold'em equity.

### Full description
Poker Vision is an offline poker learning and hand-analysis tool designed for training, study, and casual home-game analysis.

Tap any of the seven card positions to choose exactly where a card belongs. Add cards manually, scan printed card corners with your camera, or browse the complete 52-card guide. Poker Vision identifies your current hand and estimates Texas Hold'em equity against one to five random opponents using local Monte Carlo simulation.

Features:
- Exact seven-slot card selection: two hole cards and five board cards
- Camera-assisted rank and suit recognition
- Manual card entry with clear playing-card visuals
- Complete 52-card deck guide
- Poker hand rankings and examples
- Offline win, tie, loss, and equity estimates
- One to five random opponents
- English and Arabic interface
- No account, ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases
- No real-money betting, deposits, withdrawals, or cash prizes

Camera images used for card recognition are processed on the device and are not stored by Poker Vision or uploaded as card images.

Poker Vision is an educational analysis tool. Do not use electronic assistance where venue, tournament, casino, or online-service rules prohibit it.
