# Poker Vision v0.3.0 Physical Device Acceptance

This checklist is the final real-deck validation gate for the multi-card scanner. CI validates deterministic OpenCV geometry/recognition fixtures, Android packaging, package identity and signing, but synthetic fixtures are not proof that every physical deck will scan correctly.

## Required setup

- Android device with rear camera and the v0.3.0 QA APK installed.
- Conventional high-contrast indexed poker deck.
- Even indoor lighting with limited glare.
- Cards separated rather than stacked/overlapping.

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] App opens as **Poker Vision QA** without crashing.
- [ ] Manual card entry opens with **Choose suit** first, then **Choose value**.
- [ ] Choosing a valid value saves immediately and advances to the next empty slot.
- [ ] Already-used exact cards are disabled/rejected.
- [ ] Scan opens the rear-camera preview.
- [ ] BOARD and YOUR CARDS guide zones are visible.
- [ ] Two hole cards plus three board cards can be visible in one camera view.
- [ ] Separate card rectangles appear for separated cards.
- [ ] Stable detections require repeated frames rather than one-frame guesses.
- [ ] A wrong/uncertain detected card can be tapped and corrected with the shared suit → value picker.
- [ ] Confirm All remains disabled while a proposal is uncertain, duplicate or invalid.
- [ ] Confirm All atomically fills the intended table slots.
- [ ] Rescan clears pending scan proposals without deleting already-confirmed table cards.
- [ ] Closing/backgrounding the scanner stops CameraX analysis.
- [ ] Equity calculation works for 2 through 10 total players.
- [ ] English/Arabic switching updates scanner and picker controls.

## Scope limits

v0.3.0 targets conventional indexed poker cards. Stacked/heavily overlapping cards, face-down cards, novelty/decorative decks, chip/bet recognition and opponent-hole-card inference are outside this release.
